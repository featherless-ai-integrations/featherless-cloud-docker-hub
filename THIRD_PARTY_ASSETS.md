# Third-party brand assets

These marks identify the upstream software included in Featherless Cloud
images. They are reproduced without recoloring or distortion.

- `rocm-pytorch/icon.svg`: PyTorch flame from
  `pytorch/pytorch/docs/source/_static/img/pytorch-logo-flame.svg`.
  PyTorch is distributed under its BSD-style license.
- `rocm-sgl/icon.png`: SGLang square logo from
  `sgl-project/sglang/assets/logo_square.png`.
  SGLang is distributed under Apache License 2.0.
- `rocm-vllm/icon.svg`: compact vLLM logo from
  `vllm-project/media-kit/vLLM-Logo.svg`, used according to that media kit's
  guidance for compact placements.

The project names and logos remain trademarks of their respective owners and
are used only to identify the corresponding official container template.
